# Coming from Strudel

*What transfers, what is spelled differently, and what does not — on one page, and
every claim on it checked against Strudel itself. The notation is taken; the
combinators are refused ([MAP.md](MAP.md) §9.0). This page is the notation half.*

---

## The one real difference

**A Strudel step is a word; a step here is one character.** `"bd sd"` is two steps
in Strudel; `>kick x.x.` is four here, and spaces are for the eye. So a name is not
a step — it is the lane: `>kick`, `>bass`, `>disc`, and yours (`>conga = note 63`).
What a note *means* is the lane's binding; what the pattern says is when and how
much.

**A cycle is the lane's own length.** Strudel squeezes every step of a pattern into
one bar. Here each top-level character is a sixteenth, so `x.x.` is a quarter note
long and `x...x...x...x...` is a bar. Inside the line, everything means what it
means in Strudel.

## The same, spelled the same

| | Strudel | here |
|---|---|---|
| subdivide | `[x x]` | `[xx]` |
| alternate | `<a b>` | `<ab>` — a member is one step |
| chord | `[0,4,7]` | `[0,4,7]` |
| rest | `~` or `-` | `.` |
| hold | `x _ _` | `x__` |

## The same, spelled differently

| | Strudel | here |
|---|---|---|
| maybe | `x?` · `x?0.3` | `x%50` · `x%70` — the chance it *plays* |
| repeat | `x!3` | `xxx` |
| faster | `x*2` · `[a b]*2` | `[xx]` · `[abab]` |
| longer | `x@3` | `x__` |
| feet | `a b . c d e` | `[ab][cde]` |
| lane speed | `.slow(2)` — a function there | `x.x. /2` at the end — not corpus-checked |
| play n times | — | `x.x. !4`, then `name:end` |

## Not here, and why

| | why |
|---|---|
| `x(3,8)` Euclid | [MAP.md](MAP.md) §9.2, still open — joint most-used thing on this list |
| `<a b>/2` | one note over two passes; a note here ends with its lane's pass |
| `x:3` | which sample is the receiver's business ([MAP.md](MAP.md) §9.4) |
| `[a b]@2` | a tie holds a note here; Strudel stretches the whole group |
| `a \| b` | a random *choice* is a function, not notation |
| `{a b c, d e}` | two lengths are two lanes here — which is polymeter already |
| `<a b>*2` | a faster alternation is a function |
| `every`, `jux`, `off`… | functions, not notation ([MAP.md](MAP.md) §9.0) |

## Measured

`tools/corpus/strudel.txt` is Strudel's own output for every piece of the notation
— generated by `tools/corpus/gen.mjs` from Strudel 1.2.6 — and CI compiles each
translation with the deck's shipping compiler and compares every note, time and
length over twelve cycles. **All 51 in-scope patterns match.** The compiler before
the step decision ([MANIFESTO.md](MANIFESTO.md) §3.6) disagreed on 13 of them:
nested alternation, every chord, and odds on a group.

Run over the **216 distinct patterns in Strudel's own example tunes**, 2026-09-25:
**145 translate (67 %), and all 145 play Strudel's rhythm exactly.** Of the 71 that
do not: Euclid 17, `/n` on a step 17, sample index 14, `@` on a group 7, `|` 6,
`@` inside `<>` 5, `*` on `<>` 4, a fractional weight 1. Those tunes are other
people's work, so their strings are not committed; the generator reads them from a
Strudel checkout when it is run.

The odds were measured separately, since the same odds do not give the same notes
twice: over 8,000 steps Strudel played `x?0.3` 69.8 % of the time and `x?` 50.0 %,
which is `x%70` and `x%50`.

The corpus also corrected this page's author once: `<a b>/2` was first translated
as `<a a b b>`, and Strudel played half as many notes.
