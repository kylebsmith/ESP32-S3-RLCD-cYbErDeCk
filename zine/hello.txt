HELLO  zine #0
for a box that plays text.
the headings are lines too:
run one. the deck answers.

>what
a keyboard, a screen that
needs daylight, a clock.
a line is a lane. lanes
play: drums, synths,
filters, pictures.
no mouse. no menu. no mode.

>hello
>kick 9...8...9...8...
>play
ctrl+enter runs a line.
enter makes one.
the same line again: gone.
again: back.
it sends midi, not sound.
usb on   to a computer
din 17   to a synth
drums 10 bass 1 lead 2
pad 3 arp 4

>grammar
x hit  . rest  0-9 how much
_ hold   [xx] two in one
[0,4,7]  all at once
<a b>    one each time
x%30     30 in 100
/2 *2    this lane's speed
!4       four, then end

>conga
